Our story

A small cafe for slow mornings.

Slow Pour Club started with one kettle, one dripper and the idea that coffee tastes better when you take your time with it. We roast in small batches with farms we know by name, and we brew every cup to order.

Come in for a quick espresso or stay all afternoon. The Wi-Fi is fast, the chairs are comfortable, and there's always something warm out of the oven.
