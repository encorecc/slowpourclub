Our story

Not just coffee.
A slower way to enjoy it.

Slow Pour Club is about slowing down, discovering unique flavours and making every cup a personal experience. We're a small home café, so we keep things simple: a few signature drinks, done carefully, at prices that feel fair.

01

Specialty filter coffee, crafted with care

Carefully selected beans with distinctive flavour profiles, hand-brewed pour-over by pour-over. Slow brewing is the whole point.

02

Pick your mug, personalise your experience

Dine in and choose your favourite from our curated shelf of Japanese-inspired ceramics. No two visits look the same.

03

A Japanese-inspired neighbourhood hideout

A cosy, intimate space in Alexandra's HDB heartlands. A quiet escape from the city's hustle, just around the corner.

Our mug shelf

Dine-in ritual

Every cup gets its own mug.

Choosing a mug is half the fun. Here's how a visit with us goes:

  1. Choose your brewAsk what's on pour-over this week, or go for a hojicha or matcha latte.
  2. Pick a mug from the shelfHandmade, Japanese-inspired ceramics. Pick the one that matches your mood.
  3. Slow down & snap a photoStay a while. If you post it, tag @slowpourclub so we can see it.

🌿 Taking away? Bring your own cup and get S$0.50 off any drink.
